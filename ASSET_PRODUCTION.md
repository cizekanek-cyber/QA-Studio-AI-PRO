# Original collection production
Target 50–100 individually approved assets per theme, not current inventory. Animals are grouped; people and fantasy characters are separate. For cutouts use genuine transparent PNG, one subject per file. Premium photographic and cinematic light; no third-party brand marks. Never inflate inventory with crops/recolors or placeholders.
Photo manifest entries: {id,category,file,name}, category is the editor category ID. Cutout entries: {id,category,file,name,hd,width,height}; categories zvirata, lide, postavy, auta, motorky, predmety, technika, produkty, bydleni, priroda. Paths relative to the respective manifest directory. Original manifests start empty. Public manifests are for public assets only; paid assets require private storage and verified access. Validate files, licenses and transparency before publishing.
